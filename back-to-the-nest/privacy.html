Back to the Nest

Privacy Policy
Effective date: 19 November 2026

No data collected · No ads · No in-app purchases · Works offline

Back to the Nest (“the Game”) is a family game for Android created by David Guy. This policy explains how the Game handles information.

No data is collected

The Game does not collect, store on any server, or share any personal information. It does not need an internet connection, and it contains no advertising, analytics, tracking, or third-party code that collects personal information.

What stays on your device

Game progress is saved only on the device you play on, in the app’s private storage. That includes stars, unlocked outfits and shop items, stickers, best times, the player name picked from a built-in list (never a typed-in name), control and difficulty settings, and sound settings. It is never sent to us or to anyone else. Uninstalling the app deletes it.

Children

The Game is designed for children and families. Because it collects no personal data and does not use the internet, it does not seek or share any child’s personal information. A simple grown-up check protects the settings area.

Permissions

The app does not request internet access. It does not access contacts, location, photos, camera, or microphone. The optional tilt controls read the phone’s motion sensor on the device only, and no sensor data ever leaves the phone.

Changes to this policy

If the Game ever changes how it handles information, this page will be updated, along with the effective date above.

Contact

If you have questions about this policy, please contact David Guy at info@countyplastics.co.uk.
